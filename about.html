Limón/About us

Our Story

Six seats, one fridge and a crate of lemons. Seven years later, the room is bigger — the idea isn't.

How it started

Cooking with fire, salt and acid

Limón opened in 2019 as a counter on Tahlia Street: one grill, a rice cooker and whatever the fish market had left at noon. We squeezed lemons on everything because it made the food taste alive — people noticed.

Today there are forty-two seats and a proper kitchen, but the rules are the same. Short menu. Honest produce. A hot grill. A dark room so the plates can do the talking.

Timeline

Seven years in lemons

2019

Six seats on Tahlia St.

A counter, a charcoal grill and a handwritten menu of four bowls. The first poke sells out by 8pm.

2020

Takeaway through the window

The dining room closes; the window stays open. Tacos wrapped in paper keep the lights on.

2022

The room next door

We knock through the wall, install the long bar and grow to forty-two seats — and never one more.

2024

The wine list

Natural, skin-contact and low-intervention bottles arrive, chosen to stand up to all that acid.

2026

Still squeezing

Fourteen people, one grill, and more than a thousand lemons a week. Come say hello.

What we believe

Three house rules

They are written on the wall above the pass. Nobody has argued with them yet.

Fire first

Almost everything touches the grill. Char is a seasoning, and we use it generously.

Acid always

A squeeze of lemon, a spoon of pickle, a splash of vinegar. Brightness is what makes you take another bite.

Market led

We buy from the same fishermen and growers every week and write the specials around what they bring.
